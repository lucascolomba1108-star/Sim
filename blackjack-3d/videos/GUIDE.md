# Interactive Blackjack: videos

Every hand has three Reels (1080x1920, 30 fps, no audio: add music in Edits) and a cover image for each (`*_cover.jpg`, upload it as the Reel cover).

## Posting order (per hand)
1. Post `handNN_2_hit.mp4` and `handNN_3_stand.mp4` (the two decision reels).
2. In Edits, open `handNN_1_episode.mp4` and drop those two reels into the end-card slots (HIT on the left, STAND on the right). From hand 2 on, put the previous reels in the PREVIOUSLY corner slot.
3. Post the episode. If your account can link reels, link it to both decision reels.

## Slot positions (pixels on the 1080x1920 frame)
- PREVIOUSLY (corner): x 36-216, y 262-582 (180x320, 9:16). Episode: 0.2s-7.6s. HIT/STAND reels: from 0.2s until the lesson card (time listed per hand below).
- HIT (end card, left): x 130-510, y 560-1236 (380x676, 9:16). From 11.5s to the end (15.0s).
- STAND (end card, right): x 570-950, y 560-1236 (380x676). From 11.7s to the end.
- Poll sticker (optional): during "HIT or STAND?" (7.6s-11.0s) the middle of the table, y ~760-1000, is left free for it.
